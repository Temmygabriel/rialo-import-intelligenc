# MVP Scope

## Included

- Modern mobile-first landing page.
- Product URL analysis endpoint with marketplace detection.
- Graceful unavailable state for marketplace extraction.
- Evidence-based supplier intelligence card.
- Sea and air landed-cost estimates with ranges, statuses, confidence, sources, assumptions, and warnings.
- Procurement record creation and auditable state transitions.
- Mock Rialo adapter.
- Tests for marketplace, product normalization, freight math, cost aggregation, supplier scoring, procurement transitions, and Rialo abstraction.

## Excluded

- Real payment processing.
- Escrow.
- Customs brokerage.
- Warehouse operations.
- Freight booking.
- Paid marketplace scraping/API integrations.
- Complex authentication.
- Admin dashboard.
- Browser-side Rialo wallet integration.
