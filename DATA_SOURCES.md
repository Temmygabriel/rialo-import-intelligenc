# Data Sources

All MVP rates are local configurable reference data.

## Files

- `data/freight/sea-rates.json`: LCL sea freight reference rates by China origin to Lagos.
- `data/freight/air-rates.json`: air cargo reference kg rates with minimum chargeable weight and volumetric divisor.
- `data/customs/reference-rates.json`: demo customs/VAT estimation ranges by category.
- `data/destinations/nigeria.json`: clearing and local-delivery allowances for Nigerian destinations.

## Important limitations

- Data is not live.
- Data is not a quote.
- Customs values are not official assessments.
- HS classification must be confirmed before relying on duties/taxes.
- Forwarders, clearing agents, and warehouse partners must confirm real commercial terms.
